## Overview

LaiTEX is an AI-powered LaTeX document generation agent that revolutionizes the way you create professional documents. Instead of manually writing complex LaTeX code, simply describe what you want in natural language, and LaiTEX intelligently transforms your ideas into beautifully formatted, publication-ready LaTeX documents.

Whether you're an academic researcher, student, technical writer, or professional, LaiTEX streamlines the document creation process by combining the power of artificial intelligence with the precision and beauty of LaTeX typesetting.

---

## Core Features

### 🤖 Natural Language to LaTeX Conversion
Describe your document content in plain English, and LaiTEX automatically generates the corresponding LaTeX code. No need to memorize complex syntax or spend hours formatting.

### 📐 Intelligent Document Structure Generation
Automatically create well-organized document structures with proper sections, subsections, and logical hierarchy. LaiTEX understands academic and professional document conventions to ensure optimal organization.

### ∑ Mathematical Formula Creation from Descriptions
Transform mathematical descriptions into properly formatted LaTeX equations. Describe complex formulas in words, and LaiTEX renders them as professional mathematical notation.

### 📚 Bibliography and Citation Management
Seamlessly manage citations and bibliographies. LaiTEX supports multiple citation styles (APA, Chicago, Harvard, etc.) and automatically formats your references.

### 🎨 Template Customization and Styling
Choose from pre-built templates or customize styling to match your requirements. Adjust fonts, colors, margins, and layouts without touching raw LaTeX code.

### 🔄 Real-time Compilation and Preview
Get instant previews of your documents as you work. LaiTEX compiles LaTeX in real-time, allowing you to see exactly how your document will look before finalizing it.
